Regional city capture cells in city_province_cells.json

The capture geometry for these countries is dissolved using the same Natural
Earth Admin 1 region/region-code groupings used by aggregate_provinces.py:
Azerbaijan, Burkina Faso, France, Hungary, Italy, Latvia, Malta, North
Macedonia, Philippines, Slovenia, Uganda, United Kingdom, and Vietnam.

Each dissolved region is associated with game city markers located inside it.
If none fall inside, the region is assigned to its nearest game city using the
region's area-weighted centroid. A nearby territory-specific marker is
preferred for remote French regions. All exterior and interior rings are
retained, including disconnected islands.

Source: Natural Earth Admin 1,
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson
License: Public domain
Attribution: Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)

Regenerate from the Natural Earth Admin 1 GeoJSON:
python apps/scripts/generate_regional_city_cells.py path/to/ne_10m_admin_1.json

The generator updates the regions and cityRegions sections of
city_province_cells.json while preserving its cityCells section. City markers
assigned to regional capture geometry are removed from cityCells so the same
territory is not drawn twice.
