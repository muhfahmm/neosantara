Taiwan regional boundaries stored in city_province_cells.json under cityCells

Taipei, Taichung, and Kaohsiung cells each represent an entire map region,
not the individual municipality. Administrative polygons are merged using
the two existing Taiwan regional divider lines in provinces_lines.geojson:

- North (Taipei): Taipei City, New Taipei City, Keelung City, Taoyuan,
  Hsinchu, Hsinchu City, and Yilan.
- Central (Taichung): Miaoli, Taichung City, Changhua, Yunlin, Chiayi,
  Chiayi City, Nantou, and Hualien.
- South (Kaohsiung): Tainan City, Kaohsiung City, Pingtung, and Taitung.

Source: Natural Earth Admin 1, ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson
